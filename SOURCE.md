## What RolxDesk is

Open-source multi-LLM chat desk on Vercel:

- Models: OpenRouter, Gemini, Venice, custom/9router
- Modes: Normal, Debate, Teamwork
- Tools: browse, search, YouTube mini-player, image gen (Gemini), code sandbox
- extras.js: Working panel (step-by-step), anti-sticky context, connector hints, free YT search via Piped
